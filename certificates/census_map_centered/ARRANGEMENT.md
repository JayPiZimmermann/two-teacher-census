# The centered census arrangement and its visual face pairs

**Object.** The planar arrangement, in the map coordinates `(beta, y)`, of the
certified boundary set of the CENTERED two-student census: the two certified
curves of `cert/CERTIFICATE.md` (torque lens, potential curve) together with
the degenerate strata `beta = 0`, `beta = pi/2`, `beta = pi`, `y = 0`,
`y = +-1`; its faces; a certified interior point and a certified census for
every face; and the agreement with the shipped map of `website/`.

**Definitions.** The map coordinates are those of `website/widgets.js`:
`beta in [0, pi]` is the teacher gap, and `y in (-1, 1]` encodes the teacher
mass pair through `psi = (y+1) pi/2`, `(s0, s1) = (sin psi, cos psi)`
(`massesAt`).  Negating both masses is an exact isometry of the loss, so
`(s0, s1)` and `(-s0, -s1)` are one point of the map (`ratioCoord` folds by
it); consequently `y = -1` and `y = +1` are the same line and `y` lives on a
circle of circumference 2.  The sector `y in (-1, 0)` has both masses
positive ("same-sign"), the sector `y in (0, 1)` has `s0 > 0 > s1`
("mixed-sign").  The kernel atoms `phiCos` (written `phi`), `couplingH`
(written `h`), the slope atom `sA = phi - 2|sin|` and the three mass-free
determinants

```
   Wtau(beta,t) = h(t) sA(t-beta) - h(t-beta) sA(t)          torque Wronskian
   Wpot(beta,t) = phi(t) h(t-beta) - phi(t-beta) h(t)        potential Wronskian
   Wwgt(beta,t) = |sin t| h(t-beta) - |sin(t-beta)| h(t)     weight Wronskian
```

are defined, validated against both the Lean definitions and `widgets.js`, and
certified in `cert/CERTIFICATE.md`; this document consumes its results.  The
*census signature* of a teacher is the string `widgets.js censusSignature`
computes: the sorted multiset of `(family kind, proved type)` over the rows of
the classification, e.g. `coincident:trap@mixed | fit:global`.

**Precision and validity domain.** Every enclosure comes from `mpmath.iv` at
**200 bits** with directed rounding (`mp.prec = 220`), through the kernel
module `cert/centered.py`.  Second-variation tests run in `mpmath` at **60
decimal digits**.  `float64` appears only as a *locator* — a place to start a
high-precision solve — and never carries a verdict.  The domain is
`beta in [0, pi]`, `y in R/2Z`, centered kernel only (`pi`-periodic in both
`t` and `beta`), two teacher atoms and two students.  `website/` files are
inputs to the certificate scripts; regeneration writes only this certificate
directory.

## 1. Why the census is a function of the three determinants

This is the bridge between the certificate (which is about zero sets of
determinants) and the census (which is about the classification's rows), and
it is what makes a *certified* per-face census possible.

At a torque root `t` of the teacher `(beta, s0, s1)` — a zero of
`A(t) = -(s0 h(t) + s1 h(t-beta))` — and provided the kernel vector
`(-h(t-beta), h(t))` is nonzero, the mass direction is
`(s0, s1) = kappa (-h(t-beta), h(t))` for a nonzero real `kappa`.
Common-zero points on the degenerate columns are treated separately and do
not define this ratio coordinate.
Substituting into the teacher potential `P`, the transverse weight `W` and the
torque slope combination `tau = P - 2W`,

```
   P(t)   = s0 phi(t)  + s1 phi(t-beta)  = -kappa Wpot(beta,t)
   W(t)   = s0|sin t|  + s1|sin(t-beta)| = -kappa Wwgt(beta,t)
   tau(t) = P(t) - 2 W(t)                = +kappa Wtau(beta,t)
```

the last using the Lean identity `Wtau + Wpot = 2 Wwgt`
(`torqueAtomWronskian_add_potentialAtomWronskian`,
`Planar/GeneralTeachers/PotentialBoundary.lean`).  `widgets.js`
`coincidenceTypeAt` calls a coincidence row a spurious minimum iff
`tau*P > 0` and `W a b tau < 0`, with split weights `a b > 0` for the
same-sign split and `a b < 0` for the opposite one.  `kappa^2` cancels in
both products, so

```
   a trap is present at this root   <=>   Wtau * Wpot < 0
   its label is  @positive          <=>   Wtau * Wwgt > 0
   its label is  @mixed             <=>   Wtau * Wwgt < 0
```

and the exact-fit row contributes `fit:global` for every non-degenerate
teacher.  **Hence the census signature is a function of the SIGN CHART of the
teacher: the number of torque roots, and the sign of each of `Wtau`, `Wpot`,
`Wwgt` at each of them.**  In the centered model no other row can contribute:
the classification's `deadRows`, `nullRows`, `centeredQuarterRows` and
`centeredBeamRows` are all typed `topological saddle`, which the signature
discards.  (That the classifier is RIGHT to discard them — that none of those
configurations is a true local minimum — is the containment question of
section 7.)

The implication above deliberately excludes a common-root column, where the
kernel-vector ratio is `0/0`.  At `beta = pi/2` the two common torque roots are
exactly `t = 0` and `t = pi/2`, for every teacher mass pair.  Writing
`kappa = pi/2`, direct evaluation gives

```
 t = 0:     P = kappa s0 + s1,   W = s1,   tau = kappa s0 - s1
 t = pi/2:  P = s0 + kappa s1,   W = s0,   tau = -s0 + kappa s1.
```

These formulas, not a limiting mass-direction ratio, determine the exact
column census in section 3.  `check_map.js` checks them against the source
classifier at all open intervals and transition points and records the result
in `face_widget.json`.

Two structural consequences, both used below.

* Where the kernel vector is nonzero and `ratioCoord` is defined,
  `Wtau = h(t) h'(t-beta) - h(t-beta) h'(t)` is exactly the Jacobian of the
  covering `t -> y(t) = ratioCoord(-h(t-beta), h(t))`.  Thus its nontrivial
  interior zero set is the fold locus of that covering, and crossing the lens
  changes the root count by two.  Common-zero points on the degenerate columns
  are excluded from this statement.
* Off the degenerate `beta` columns, `Wwgt` vanishes exactly at `t = 0` and
  `t = beta`, whose images are `y = 0` and `y = +-1`.  On a degenerate column
  it can vanish identically and is treated separately. Crossing the two fixed strata flips the label
  `@positive <-> @mixed` while preserving the trap count — they are
  *relabelling* boundaries, not count boundaries.  (Under the map's `(-,-)`
  fold the representative is normalised to `s0 >= 0`; crossing `y = +-1`
  changes the sign of `s0`, so the normalisation jumps by a global negation,
  which is exactly a `positive <-> mixed` relabelling.  The mathematics is
  continuous across the seam; only the label is not.)

The certified sign chart is computed by `cert_core.sign_chart`: the torque
roots are isolated to completeness on each smooth piece (`t in [0, beta]` and
`t in [beta, pi]`, the split at the kink lattice of the certificate) by an
interval exclusion / monotonicity test finished with interval Newton, and the
three determinants are then evaluated on the root enclosure with
`centered.dets_branch` on the piece's own branch indices.

## 2. The geometry of the two curves, exactly

Write `b = beta` on piece A (`t in [beta, pi]`) and `b = pi - beta` on piece B
(`t in [0, beta]`), `W = (pi-b)/2`, `|w| <= W`, and use the certificate's
reduction `Wtau = sigma Phi`, `Wpot = sigma Psi`, `Wwgt = sigma Om` with
`Phi = Q + Om`, `Psi = -Q + Om`, `Q = (w^2 - b^2/4) sin b`,
`Om = b cos(w + b/2) cos(w - b/2)` (certificate section 2, sympy-exact).

**(a) Both curves are graphs, and where.** `Psi_w < 0` strictly on `(0, W]`
(certificate L5), so `Psi` has at most one zero there; `Psi(b,0) > 0` and
`Psi(b,W) = (pi/2)(b-pi/2) sin b`, so `Wpot` has exactly two zeros `+-w_Psi`,
on the piece with `b < pi/2`, for every `beta != pi/2`.  `Phi_w < 0` strictly
on `(0,W]` for `b > pi/2` (L7) with `Phi(b,W) < 0`, and `Phi > 0` on all of
`[0,W]` for `b < pi/2` (L8), so `Wtau` has exactly two zeros `+-w_Phi` when
`pi/2 < b < b*` and none otherwise.  Both are unique zeros of functions
certified strictly monotone at them, so both curves are `C^1` graphs over
`beta` by the implicit function theorem.

**(b) The torque lens lies strictly inside `y in (-1, 0)`, by an exact sign
argument, not a hull.** For `beta < pi/2` the `Wtau` zeros are interior to
piece B, where `h(t) = (pi/2-t) sin t > 0` (because `0 < t < beta < pi/2`) and
`h(t-beta) = (pi/2-x) sin x < 0` with `x = t-beta+pi in (pi-beta, pi)`.
Opposite signs give `s0 > 0, s1 > 0`, i.e. `psi in (0, pi/2)`, i.e.
`y in (-1,0)`.  For `beta > pi/2` the same computation on piece A gives
`h(t) < 0`, `h(t-beta) > 0`.  So the lens never meets `y = 0` or `y = +-1`.

**(c) The potential curve lies strictly inside `y in (0, 1)`, likewise.** On
piece A with `b = beta < pi/2` the sub-interval of `t` that maps to
`y in (-1,0)` is exactly `|w| < b/2`.  And `Psi > 0` for `|w| <= b/2`, because
there `-Q = (b^2/4 - w^2) sin b >= 0` and `Om >= 0` with `Om > 0` strictly
(as `b/2 < pi/4 < W` for `b < pi/2`).  Since `Psi` is strictly decreasing in
`|w|`, its zero satisfies `w_Psi in (b/2, W)`, i.e. the root lies in the part
of the piece that maps into `y in (0,1)`.

**(d) They are disjoint from each other** (immediate from (b) and (c)), **each
meets `beta = pi/2` in exactly two points** (at `beta = pi/2` all three
determinants have the same two zeros `t in {0, pi/2}`, on the kink lattice,
and the two limits of `y` are distinct: `-c, c-1` for the torque curve and
`c, 1-c` for the potential one, `c = (2/pi) arctan(2/pi)`), **the lens closes
at its two double roots** `(beta1*, -1/2)` and `(beta2*, -1/2)`, **and the
potential curve closes at** `(0, 1/2)` and `(pi, 1/2)` (its two branches
satisfy `y_upper = 1 - y_lower` by the mirror `t -> beta - t`, so they are
distinct for every `beta` in `(0,pi)` and meet only in the limit).

So the torque lens is a Jordan curve bounding a disk inside `y in (-1,0)`, and
the potential curve is two disjoint arcs crossing the strip `y in (0,1)` from
wall to wall.

## 3. The arrangement and the face count

**Domain.** `y` is a circle of circumference 2, so the map is a closed
**cylinder**, not a rectangle.  `beta = 0` and `beta = pi` are its two
boundary circles; because the kernel is `pi`-periodic in `beta` they are the
same stratum, and identifying them turns the cylinder into a torus.  The ten
open-face labels are unchanged under that identification because `beta = 0`
is already included among the certified strata.

**Cell counts.**

```
   vertices  V = 14      exactly the 14 zero-dimensional pieces 0D-1 .. 0D-14
   edges     E = 24      the 9 distinct curves cut at those vertices
   faces     F = 10
   V - E + F = 0 = chi(annulus)                                   [Euler check]
```

The nine distinct curves are the two lens branches, the two potential
branches, `y = 0` (= the certificate's 1D-5 and 1D-9, which coincide),
`y = +-1` (= 1D-6 and 1D-10), and the three columns `beta = 0, pi/2, pi`.
They are cut into `4 + 4 + 2 + 2 + 3 + 6 + 3 = 24` edges.  Under the torus
identification `V = 11`, `E = 21`, `F = 10`, and `V - E + F = 0 = chi(torus)`.

**The ten faces, with a certified interior point and the certified census.**
Every interior point is an exactly-representable `float64` value, so its
interval is degenerate and the whole chart below is certified.  `n` is the
certified number of torque roots.

At `y = 0` and the seam `y = +-1`, one teacher mass vanishes and only global
representations remain.  The source key `trap@positive` is historical: on
this column it means a same-sign student split.  In the upper part of the
column that split can consist of two negative masses, so the key must not be
read literally as “both student masses positive.”  `face_widget.json` records
all ten representatives above (including the four threshold cases) and the
mass-zero intersections; every source-classifier check agrees.

On `beta = 0` the teachers merge into one effective atom, except at
`y = 1/2`, where their masses cancel and the target field is zero.  The column
`beta = pi` is the same projective stratum, not a second boundary condition:
the source regression gives identical signatures at both drawings, including
`coincident:global | dead:global` at the cancelling point.  The horizontal
strata `y = 0` and `y = +-1` likewise have one effective teacher and no trap.

The two curved strata carry a one-trap census.  On the
torque lens the row whose `tau` vanishes is non-strict and is discarded, while
the other strict root leaves `coincident:trap@mixed | fit:global`.  On the
potential curve the row whose pinned total mass vanishes is a saddle, while
the other root leaves `coincident:trap@positive | fit:global`.  At their four
meetings with `beta = pi/2`, these are exactly the threshold rows of the table,
so the exact-column signatures match the relevant open-face signatures instead
of becoming fit-only.

For the figure and renderer comparison we record exactly five **visual face
pairs**:

| pair | open faces | local exact-column witness | shared census |
|---|---|---|---|
| VP1 | F1, F4 | `y in (-1,c-1)` and `(-c,0)` | one `trap@mixed` |
| VP2 | F2, F3 | `y in (c-1,-c)` | two `trap@mixed` |
| VP3 | F5, F8 | `y in (0,c)` | one `trap@positive` |
| VP4 | F6, F9 | `y in (c,1-c)` | two `trap@positive` |
| VP5 | F7, F10 | `y in (1-c,1)` | one `trap@positive` |

This is an explicitly non-topological relation.  `build_arrangement.py`
checks equality of the two face signatures; `check_map.js` checks the exact
pair labels and the listed local `beta = pi/2` source witnesses.  Neither
checker establishes connectivity, adjacency, or the exclusion of another
path.  The five labels must therefore not be called regions or connected
components of a census level set.

## 4. The shipped finite-grid map and the pinch no grid can resolve

The generator (`website/precompute_census_map.js`) samples the classification
on a dyadic grid and floods equal-census cells into finite-grid groups.  Those
groups are renderer output, not inferred connected components of the exact
map.

**The visual pairing across `beta = pi/2` is source-consistent.**  The five
labelled pairs F1|F4, F2|F3, F5|F8, F6|F9, F7|F10 have equal face signatures,
and each has matching listed local exact-column witnesses.  The four transition
points retain the corresponding one-trap signature after the non-strict
candidate drops out.  A grid never samples the exact column.  These checks
justify the displayed pair labels; they do not prove that the paired faces lie
in one connected census-level component.

**The pinch is scale-invariant, so refinement alone cannot keep the two outer
one-`POS` face bands visually separate.**  The potential curve has two
distinct branches over all of `0 < beta < pi` and they meet in the limit at
the boundary points `(0, 1/2)` and `(pi, 1/2)`.  Near `beta = 0`
the lens width in `y` tends to `0.28113816169 * beta` (measured at 50 digits;
the limit is approached from below, `width/beta = 0.281138110` at
`beta = 10^-3`), while the first column of a depth-`d` grid sits at
`beta = pi/(48 * 2^d)` with cells `1/(6 * 2^d)` tall — both scale like
`2^-d`, so the pinch is `0.110403` cells wide at every depth (measured
constant to six digits over depths 4-16).  A flood fill therefore walks
around the tip at any resolution.  The fix shipped in commit `f430fab`:
the generator takes the certified potential curve as a SEPARATOR — it solves
the reduced form `Psi(b, w) = 0` on the certified bracket `w in (0, W)` once
per column (the double-precision solve reproduces
`cert_core.curve_branches(beta, "potential")` to 15 significant digits at
every `beta` tested), carries the two roots to `y` through the shipped
`ratioCoord`, labels every cell by which of the three bands its centre lies
in, and unions two neighbours only when their census AND their label agree.
The torque lens is NOT used as a separator: it spans only
`beta in [beta1*, beta2*]`, so it has no certified full-width presentation
contract.  Injecting a partial separator would split a finite-grid group
without such a convention.  The generator therefore refuses a separator
curve that has no branch in some column.

The historical measurements that identified the two defects of the 8-piece
asset (commit `72fb166`) are kept in `arrangement.json`:
`mosaic_comparison` (the pinch arithmetic and the piece outlines of that
asset) and `sel_eps_artifact_band` (the measured `y`-width, per `beta`, of
the band on which the then-absolute cutoff `SEL_EPS = 1e-7` reported
`fit:global` over a genuine trap — above one depth-8 cell exactly for
`beta < ~0.0125` and `beta > ~3.129`, matching the four slivers' measured
extent, and nonzero at every `beta`, which is why the count's stability
across depths 6-8 was never evidence of convergence).

## 5. The count collars: closed by exact inequalities

Certificate section 9.1 leaves six `beta` collars, total measure `4.712e-13`,
around `beta1*`, `pi/2`, `beta2*`, on which a root COUNT was not certified by
the generic box test (whose margin must beat the box width, and degenerates
at the critical betas).  They close, and so does the entire `beta` axis,
because the counts are decided by **exact inequalities on the reduced
forms**:

```
   C1  Om = b cos(w+b/2) cos(w-b/2) >= 0, zero exactly at |w| = W.
       For every nondegenerate beta, Wwgt has exactly two zeros in t
       (at t = 0 and t = beta); on degenerate beta columns it can vanish
       identically and is handled separately.

   C2  Psi_w = -(2w sin b + b sin 2w) < 0 on (0,W]; Psi(b,0) > 0;
       Psi(b,W) = (pi/2)(b - pi/2) sin b.
       -> Wpot has 2 zeros on the piece with b < pi/2 and none on the other,
          for every b != pi/2.

   C3  b > pi/2: D := -Phi_w = b sin 2w - 2w sin b has D(0)=0, D_ww <= 0 on
       0 <= 2w <= pi-b, D(W) = (2b-pi) sin b > 0, so D > 0 on (0,W] and Phi
       is strictly decreasing; Phi(b,W) = (pi/4)(pi-2b) sin b < 0.  The count
       is decided by sign Phi(b,0) = sign of g(b) = cos(b/2) - (b/2) sin(b/2).

   C4  b < pi/2: Phi > 0 on [0,W], with NO case analysis at b = pi/2:
         on [0, b/2]  D(0) = 0 and D(b/2) = 0 with D concave there, so
                      D >= 0, Phi_w <= 0 and Phi >= Phi(b,b/2) = b cos b > 0;
         on [b/2, W]  Q >= 0 and Om >= 0, and with w_m = (b/2 + W)/2,
                      Phi >= Q(w_m) > 0 for w >= w_m and Phi >= Om(w_m) > 0
                      for w <= w_m.

   C5  g'(b) = -sin(b/2) - (b/4) cos(b/2) < 0 on (0,pi), so g is strictly
       decreasing and its unique zero b* separates the two cases of C3 for
       every b != b*.
```

`C4`'s first half is a cleaner proof than the certificate's L8 (which routes
through `(pi/2 - p) tan p < 1` and degenerates as `b -> pi/2`), and it is what
lets the `pi/2` collars close.

Machine check (`collars.py`, adaptive interval cover, refinement floor
`1e-45`): the six collars certify with residual at most `1.12e-45` each, and
a full cover of `beta in [1e-12, pi - 1e-12]` certifies
`(Wtau, Wpot, Wwgt) = (0,2,2)` outside `[beta1*, beta2*]` and `(2,2,2)`
inside, with total residual measure **5.763e-45** (`collars.json`; counts
are roots in `t` per `pi`-period, as in certificate section 4).

The residual uncertified set is exactly the three `beta` VALUES `beta1*`,
`pi/2`, `beta2*`.  That is not a defect of the method: at those points the
deciding inequality's margin is `O(distance to the critical value)`, so *no*
interval method can certify at the point, and the `1e-45` figure is only the
refinement floor.  Each of the three is handled exactly in certificate
section 5.  A single `beta` value contains no open face and therefore cannot
supply an additional two-dimensional open face away from the certified
boundary curves.

## 6. The quarter family on `y = -1/2`

While enumerating separated critical points a **fourth mass-free
determinant** appeared, and it is on the record because the certificate's
boundary set does not contain it.

A separated critical point with `D = th0 - th1 = pi/2 (mod pi)` has
`h(D) = 0`, so the torque rows cannot be solved for the masses.  Instead
**both** students must sit at zeros of the teacher torque and the masses come
from the radial rows.  Such a family exists exactly on the zero set of

```
   G(beta, t) = h(t-beta) h(t+pi/2) - h(t) h(t+pi/2-beta),
```

and enumerating `{G = 0}` (399 betas, 40 001 `t`-samples each, every root
confirmed at 50 digits; `quarter_locus.py`) its image in the map is exactly

```
   { y = 0 }   u   { y = +-1 }   u   { y = -1/2 }
```

The first two are already strata of the arrangement (`s1 = 0` makes `t = 0`
and `t = pi/2` both torque roots, a quarter turn apart; `s0 = 0` does the
same for `t = beta` and `t = beta + pi/2`).  The third is the equal-mass
line: for `s0 = s1` the bisector `beta/2` and its perpendicular
`beta/2 + pi/2` are both torque roots.

**This family is never a local minimum — proved in Lean.**
`genQuarterBranch_eqTeacherMass_not_isLocalMin`
(`Planar/GeneralTeachers/Benign/GenQuarter/EqTeacherMass.lean`) states: for
`0 < beta < pi`, `beta != pi/2`, equal teacher masses `(s, s)` with `s > 0`,
every configuration with the two students a quarter turn apart mod `pi`, both
at torque zeros, and the weights solving the radial pair (`GenQuarterBranch`)
is not a local minimum of the pair loss.  At `beta = pi/2` the quarter points
include exact fits, so an all-saddle statement is impossible there; the
`beta = pi/2` column is the degenerate stratum of section 3 either way.
Hence the line `y = -1/2` is NOT a census boundary and the faces F1 and F4
that it crosses are not split.

An independent interval certificate corroborates this on
`beta in [0.002, pi - 0.002]` (`quarter_saddle.py`, 22 504 adaptive boxes, 0
failures): the angular block of the Hessian — with `h(D) = 0` the mass/angle
coupling vanishes and the Hessian is block diagonal — has

```
   det A = c0 c1 [Tau0 Tau1 + c0 Tau0 + c1 Tau1] < 0
```

certified in FACTORED form on every box (the expanded `A00 A11 - A01^2`
loses everything to interval dependency: its enclosure on the box `[1, 1.1]`
is `[-2.15, +1.57]`, useless — a methodological point worth recording).  The
masses are evaluated at `(s0, s1) = (1, 1)`; scaling the teacher by `lambda`
scales `det A` by `lambda^4`, so the sign at `y = -1/2` follows.  Two
structural facts fall out: `Tau0` vanishes exactly at `beta = beta1*` and
`Tau1` exactly at `beta = beta2*` (certified enclosures `7.02e-14`,
`-1.06e-15` at the two points), so the two lens endpoints are where this
family's angular Hessian loses a diagonal entry — the quarter locus and the
torque lens are tangent to each other's degeneracies at exactly the two
double roots of `Wtau`.  `det A -> 0` like `O(beta^4)` at both ends of the
range, which is why the interval certification stops at `0.002`; the Lean
theorem has no such gap.

`widgets.js` lists this family (`centeredQuarterRows`) only under
`T.s0 === T.s1`, a `float64` equality that `massesAt(-0.5)` never satisfies,
so the shipped map never draws it — correctly, since it changes no census.

## 7. Containment: what makes the classifier's census the true census

Everything above certifies the census **as `widgets.js` computes it**.  It
becomes a statement about the *true* multiset of local-minimum families
exactly when the classifier's row typing is the truth.  The Lean tree now
carries this, with the following scope.

**Same-sign teachers (`y < 0`; both masses positive after the fold), student
weights and angles completely free.**  Machine-checked and unconditional for
`0 < beta < pi`, `s0, s1 > 0`:

* `centered_twoTeacher_signed_localMin_iff_global_or_localMaxOuterSplit`
  (`Planar/GeneralTeachers/Convenience/GeneralMassSignedLocalMinIff.lean`;
  the `L_C` form is `L_C_twoStudent_localMin_iff_global_or_localMaxOuterSplit`
  in `Main/TwoStudentMinima.lean`): a configuration is a local minimum iff it
  is a global minimum or a coincident pair on a line at an actual local
  MAXIMUM of the teacher potential with OPPOSITE-signed student weights
  (`GenLineLocalMaxOuterSplit`).  Criticality, student signs, nonzero student
  masses and `beta != pi/2` are derived, not assumed.
* `centered_twoTeacher_generalMass_critical_iff_labeled_families`
  (`Planar/GeneralTeachers/Classification/FamilyLabels.lean`): the complete
  critical-point enumeration behind it — critical iff coincidence-line branch,
  quarter branch, or separated branch, with the variational label of each.

This is exactly the sign-chart census: the only nonglobal minima are
coincidence traps at potential maxima with opposite split — rows the chart
labels `trap@mixed` — and the separated/quarter/dead rows are never minima.

**Mixed-sign teachers (`y > 0`).**  The current public endpoint is stronger
than the older stratum-by-stratum inventory:

* `LeanFormalization.Main.centered_two_student_full_classification`
  (`Main/TwoStudentClassification.lean`) has no hypotheses on teacher angles,
  teacher masses, student angles, or student masses. It states
  `IsCriticalL_C beta s c theta` if and only if
  `CenteredTwoStudentPopulationClassification beta s c theta`; the latter is
  the exhaustive labeled classification together with its exact
  `L_CVariationalType`.

The following older endpoints remain useful ingredients and cross-checks for
the separated and quarter strata when `0 < beta < pi` and `s0 s1 < 0`:

* `centered_mixedSign_distinct_critical_isExactFit`
  (`Planar/GeneralTeachers/SeparatedRigidity/MixedSeparated.lean`): every
  critical configuration with the two students DISTINCT mod `pi` and both
  weights nonzero is the exact fit — students on the teacher directions and
  weights equal to the teacher masses, in one of the two orders.  So the
  mixed-sign separated stratum contributes nothing but `fit:global`.
* `centered_mixedSign_no_quarter_critical_point`
  (`Planar/GeneralTeachers/QuarterRigidity/MixedSignEmpty.lean`,
  `beta != pi/2`): no mixed-sign critical configuration has a quarter-turn
  gap with both weights alive.

The hypothesis-free public endpoint also covers coincident rows and
configurations with a zero student mass. Their variational types are therefore
Lean-proved; the interval selectors at the mixed-sign witnesses are numerical
corroboration rather than the only typing evidence.

**Numerical corroboration** (both halves, kept because it is an independent
instrument): for 6144 mixed-sign teachers on a `96 x 64` grid, a `float64`
locator + 60-digit re-solve found NO separated critical family with both
weights nonzero — none exists to find, exactly as the exact-fit theorem says
— while the positive control found the beam family for every one of 2400
same-sign teachers and typed all 2400 as saddles at 60 digits
(`sep_mixed.json`, `sep_positive.json`, `sep_fine.json`; locator sensitivity
control at two grid resolutions included).

## 8. Summary of the evidence

**Certified (interval arithmetic at 200 bits, plus the exact sign arguments
of sections 2 and 5):**

1. The certified boundary set forms an arrangement with `V = 14`, `E = 24`,
   `F = 10` in the map cylinder, Euler-consistent.
2. Each of the ten faces has the certified interior point, sign chart and
   census of section 3, constant across that face's witnesses.
3. The `Wtau`/`Wpot`/`Wwgt` root counts hold for **every** `beta` except the
   three critical values `beta1*`, `pi/2`, `beta2*`, which are handled
   exactly.  At `pi/2` the determinant count alone does not determine the mass
   ratio; the direct common-root formulas of sections 1 and 3 do.
4. The torque lens lies strictly in `y in (-1,0)` and the potential curve
   strictly in `y in (0,1)`; the curves are disjoint from each other and from
   `y = 0`, `y = +-1`.
5. The quarter family's angular block is negative-determinant on
   `[0.002, pi - 0.002]` (corroborating the Lean theorem that covers all of
   `(0, pi) \ {pi/2}`).

**Proved (Lean, all cited files compiled):** the hypothesis-free public
classification `centered_two_student_full_classification`, including the
mixed-sign coincident and zero-mass rows; the containment statements of
section 7; the linear dependence `Wtau + Wpot = 2 Wwgt`; and the
quarter-family saddle theorem of section 6.

the one/two/one trap counts in both teacher-sign sectors, all four transition

**Measured (float sampling, no interval backing):** the shipped-map
